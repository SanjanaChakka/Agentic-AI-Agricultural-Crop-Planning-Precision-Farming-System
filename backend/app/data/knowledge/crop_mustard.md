# Mustard agronomic requirements

Soil: mustard prefers loam, silt loam, clay loam, alluvium and black soils with
good drainage.

pH: optimum 6.0 to 7.5; tolerable 5.5 to 8.0.

Temperature: optimum 15 to 25 degrees C. Frost damages the crop, and terminal
heat above 30 degrees C during pod filling causes severe yield loss.

Water: season rainfall 300 to 450 mm; water requirement 250 to 400 mm. Mustard
is mostly grown on residual moisture with one irrigation at rosette or flowering.

Moisture: optimum root-zone volumetric moisture 40 to 60 percent; irrigation
trigger around 28 percent.

Critical stages: establishment, rosette, flowering and pod filling. Moisture at
seeding decides whether the crop needs a supplemental irrigation at all.

Risks: white rust and downy mildew are favoured by cool, cloudy, humid weather
with leaf wetness; alternaria blight follows wet spells; aphid infestation
builds up in mild, humid autumn weather; sarson aphid is a vector of mosaic
virus.
