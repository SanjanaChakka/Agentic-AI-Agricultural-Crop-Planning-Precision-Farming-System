# Groundnut agronomic requirements

Soil: groundnut prefers well-drained, light, friable soils - sandy loam, red
soil and sandy surfaces. Heavy clay and soils prone to waterlogging cause root
and pod rot.

pH: optimum 6.0 to 7.5; tolerable 5.5 to 8.0.

Temperature: optimum 25 to 32 degrees C. Germination and pegging are poor below
18 degrees C.

Water: season rainfall 400 to 600 mm; water requirement 350 to 500 mm. The
critical period is the 90 to 120 day pod development stage.

Moisture: optimum root-zone volumetric moisture 40 to 60 percent; irrigation
trigger around 25 percent. Pegging and pod development need consistent moisture,
and alternating drought and irrigation causes empty pods.

Critical stages: germination and establishment, flowering and pegging, pod
development and pod filling.

Risks: red rot (Aspergillus niger / Rhizoctonia) and stem rot are favoured by
warm, wet soil. Tikka leaf spot and leaf webber spread under high humidity.
Rosette virus is vectored by aphids in cool, humid conditions.
