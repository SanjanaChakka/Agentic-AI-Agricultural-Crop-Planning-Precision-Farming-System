# Pearl millet agronomic requirements

Soil: pearl millet is well suited to light, sandy and sandy loam soils, red
soils and red laterite with low organic matter. It is one of the few crops that
performs acceptably on poor, shallow soils.

pH: optimum 6.0 to 7.5; tolerable 5.5 to 8.5 - it tolerates mild salinity
better than most cereals.

Temperature: optimum 25 to 32 degrees C, tolerating up to about 42 degrees C.

Water: season rainfall 300 to 600 mm; water requirement 250 to 400 mm. It is a
low-water-demand crop suited to rainfed farming and terminal drought.

Moisture: optimum root-zone volumetric moisture 30 to 50 percent; irrigation
trigger around 20 percent.

Critical stages: emergence, tillering and grain filling.

Risks: downy mildew (systemic and green ear) is favoured by cool, cloudy, humid
weather; ergot is favoured by high humidity at flowering; downy mildew and rust
both need free moisture and moderate temperatures.
