# Cotton agronomic requirements

Soil: cotton performs well on deep black soils, alluvium, clay loam and red
soils with good drainage.

pH: optimum 6.0 to 7.5; tolerable 5.5 to 8.5.

Temperature: optimum 21 to 30 degrees C. Squaring and boll development are
impaired above 37 degrees C.

Water: season rainfall 600 to 1200 mm; total water requirement 700 to 1100 mm.
Cotton has a long season and comparatively high water demand.

Moisture: optimum root-zone volumetric moisture 45 to 70 percent; irrigation
trigger around 30 percent.

Critical stages: squaring (about 40-60 days), flowering and boll development.
Alternating moisture stress and rewatering promotes shedding, so scheduling
irrigations evenly is important.

Risks: boll rot (Xanthomonas and Alternaria) is strongly favoured by high
humidity, warm temperatures and wet canopies after rain. Pink bollworm and white
fly build up under dry, humid spells. Waterlogging damages roots and promotes
verticillium wilt.
