# Pigeonpea agronomic requirements

Soil: pigeonpea grows on black soils, red soils, loams, clay loams and
laterites. Its deep root system gives it an advantage over shallow-rooted crops
on marginal land.

pH: optimum 6.0 to 7.5; tolerable 5.5 to 8.0.

Temperature: optimum 24 to 33 degrees C. It is heat tolerant during
vegetative growth but sensitive to cold and to excess rain at flowering.

Water: season rainfall 500 to 800 mm; water requirement 350 to 500 mm. It is
drought tolerant once established and can carry a crop on residual moisture.

Moisture: optimum root-zone volumetric moisture 40 to 60 percent; irrigation
trigger around 27 percent.

Critical stages: flowering, pod development and pod filling. Waterlogging
during flowering causes heavy flower drop.

Risks: wilt and sterility rot are favoured by waterlogging and warm soil;
pod borer and pod fly incidence rises after rainfall; phytophthora blight
occurs in waterlogged heavy soils.
