# Tomato agronomic requirements

Soil: tomato grows on loam, sandy loam, silt loam, alluvial and red soils with
good drainage. Very light soils need frequent light irrigation; heavy soils
invite root diseases.

pH: optimum 6.0 to 7.0; tolerable 5.5 to 7.5.

Temperature: optimum 18 to 27 degrees C. Night temperatures above 24 degrees C
block fruit set, and frost damages the crop at any stage.

Water: season rainfall 600 to 1250 mm for a long-duration crop; water
requirement 450 to 700 mm.

Moisture: optimum root-zone volumetric moisture 55 to 75 percent; irrigation
trigger around 38 percent. Irregular moisture - alternating wet and dry -
causes fruit cracking, blossom end rot and fruit rot.

Critical stages: establishment, flowering, fruit set and fruit development.

Risks: late blight is favoured by cool, wet, humid weather; early blight and
Septoria leaf spot by warm, humid conditions with leaf wetness; bacterial wilt
builds up in warm, wet soil. Prolonged leaf wetness from overhead irrigation
increases all of these.
