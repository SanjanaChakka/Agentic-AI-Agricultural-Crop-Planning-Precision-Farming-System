# Maize agronomic requirements

Soil: maize grows on loam, sandy loam, silt loam, clay loam and alluvial soils.
Sandy soils need frequent irrigation; heavy clay restricts root growth.

pH: optimum 5.5 to 7.5; tolerable 5.0 to 8.0.

Temperature: optimum 21 to 27 degrees C. Growth stops below 10 degrees C and
pollen viability falls when temperatures exceed 35 degrees C at tasselling.

Water: season rainfall of 600 to 1200 mm; total crop water requirement 500 to
800 mm. Maize is more drought tolerant than rice or wheat at vegetative stages
but has no tolerance for water shortage between tasselling and grain filling.

Moisture: optimum root-zone volumetric moisture 50 to 75 percent; irrigation is
generally triggered below about 35 percent.

Critical stages: tasselling, silking and grain filling. Moisture stress of only
5 to 7 days at silking can reduce yield by a large fraction because it directly
reduces silk emergence and fertilisation.

Risks: stalk rot and ear rot increase after heavy rain or waterlogging.
Grey leaf spot and northern leaf blight spread under warm, humid conditions.
Fall armyworm pressure is higher in humid, mild conditions.
