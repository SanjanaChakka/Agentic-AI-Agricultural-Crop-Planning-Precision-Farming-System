# Chickpea agronomic requirements

Soil: chickpea grows on loam, silt loam, clay loam, black soil and alluvial
soils. It is sensitive to waterlogging and to saline-sodic soils.

pH: optimum 6.0 to 7.5; tolerable 5.5 to 8.0.

Temperature: optimum 15 to 25 degrees C. Vegetative growth stops near 32
degrees C and pod filling is damaged above that temperature.

Water: season rainfall 300 to 500 mm; water requirement 300 to 450 mm. Chickpea
is predominantly grown on residual moisture with one supplemental irrigation
at flowering in many regions.

Moisture: optimum root-zone volumetric moisture 40 to 60 percent; irrigation
trigger around 28 percent.

Critical stages: emergence, branching, flowering and pod filling. Moisture
shortage at flowering reduces pod number; excess moisture causes wilt and
damping off.

Risks: wilt (Fusarium) and dry root rot are favoured by warm soil and soil
moisture stress; ascochyta blight spreads in cool, wet weather; pod borer
activity follows soft growth following rain.
