# Finger millet agronomic requirements

Soil: finger millet grows on red soils, sandy loams, laterites and loams. It
tolerates poor soils better than most cereals but responds to organic matter.

pH: optimum 5.5 to 7.5; tolerable 5.0 to 8.0.

Temperature: optimum 20 to 30 degrees C. It tolerates mild heat but is sensitive
to frost.

Water: season rainfall 400 to 600 mm; water requirement 300 to 450 mm. It is
grown mainly under rainfed conditions with modest supplemental irrigation.

Moisture: optimum root-zone volumetric moisture 40 to 60 percent; irrigation
trigger around 25 percent.

Critical stages: emergence, tillering, finger initiation and grain filling.

Risks: blast is favoured by long dew periods, high humidity and cloudy weather;
brown spot and leaf rust need free moisture; blast risk is highest when the
crop is exposed to dew or mist for several consecutive days.
