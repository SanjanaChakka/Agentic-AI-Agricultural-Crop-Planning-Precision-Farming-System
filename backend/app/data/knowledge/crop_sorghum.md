# Sorghum agronomic requirements

Soil: sorghum is adapted to light, well-drained soils - sandy loam, red soils
and sandy surfaces with lower fertility - as well as black soils under
irrigation.

pH: optimum 5.5 to 7.5; tolerable 5.0 to 8.0. Among cereals sorghum tolerates
the widest pH range.

Temperature: optimum 25 to 32 degrees C. Sorghum tolerates up to about 40
degrees C, which is why it is the cereal of choice in heat-prone tracts.

Water: season rainfall 400 to 700 mm; water requirement 350 to 550 mm. It
survives a terminal dry spell better than maize or rice and is routinely grown
on residual soil moisture.

Moisture: optimum root-zone volumetric moisture 35 to 55 percent; irrigation
trigger around 25 percent.

Critical stages: emergence, booting, flowering and grain filling.

Risks: grain mould and anthracnose are favoured by warm, humid weather during
maturation; downy mildew and leaf spot appear in wet seasons; shoot fly and
stem borer incidence rises under high humidity.
